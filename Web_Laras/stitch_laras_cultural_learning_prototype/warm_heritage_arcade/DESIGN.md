## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-energy, culturally resonant gamified learning environment geared towards Gen Z (ages 15–24). The aesthetic blends **tactile microlearning mechanics** with **contemporary regional pride**, avoiding dusty museum tropes in favor of vibrant, arcade-inspired cultural discovery. 

The emotional signature is celebratory, welcoming, and deeply rooted:
- **Warm & Vibrant:** Grounded in indigenous earth tones, sun-baked terracotta, and honey golds, elevated by bright tropical accents.
- **Empowering & Gamified:** Micro-rewards, rhythmic streak systems, dual-track mastery bars, and punchy visual feedback evoke instant gratification without visual noise.
- **Modern Heritage:** Celebrates Kaili textiles and Central Sulawesi craftsmanship through clean geometric rhythms, refined organic shapes, and uncluttered mobile interfaces.

## Colors

The palette balances warm earthen terracotta with radiant solar ambers and punchy celebratory jewels, set over clean, creamy base surfaces.

- **Primary (`#E05A36` - Terracotta Ember):** Drives primary actions, progression pathways, active lesson states, and energetic identity moments. Deep variant `#C84624` is reserved for pressed button states and active border edges.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Honey Ochre):** Fuels the reward engine—XP counters, golden star badges, streak fire indicators, and rank capsules.
- **Tertiary (`#6366F1` - Sulawesi Indigo):** Signals deep heritage content, special "Culture Connection" lore cards, textile unlocks, and rare artifact milestones.
- **Gamification Emerald (`#10B981`):** Functional accent dedicated exclusively to correct answers, completed lesson milestones, and positive streak affirmations.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5` base canvas, `#FFFFFF` cards):** Creates an airy, high-contrast surface hierarchy that relieves eye fatigue while preserving legibility.
- **Typography & Structural Slates:** Deep warm charcoal (`#1F1E24`) for effortless headline scanning, softened to warm stone (`#706E7A`) for secondary metadata and tertiary prompts.

## Typography

Typography is powered across all levels by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, bringing a rounded, contemporary, and distinctly optimistic structure suited for Gen Z users. 

- **Display & Large Headlines:** Dense letterforms with a slightly tightened letter-spacing (`-0.02em`) produce punchy, arcade-style lesson headings and celebratory numbers.
- **Numbers & Counters:** Numerical readouts (XP, day counts, percentage mastery) are explicitly rendered in `700` and `800` weights to emphasize progression.
- **Body & Prompts:** Clear open counters ensure high-speed legibility when answering flashcard questions or reading dialogue transcripts.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase and tracked labels are used selectively for categories, linguistic roots, and badge identifiers to maintain tight visual hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a mobile-first fluid viewport philosophy, optimized for single-handed smartphone interactions and constrained web app shells (max content width `480px` on desktop/tablet, centered with subtle canvas borders).

- **Screen Safe Zones:** Maintain a fixed `16px` outer margin (`margin-mobile`) on standard viewport screens, stepping up to `24px` on wider phone frames.
- **Rhythm & Grid:** Built on an `8px` baseline rhythm. Lesson steps along the learning path are spaced using `space-xl` (`32px`) to accommodate animated progress connectors and path nodes.
- **Sticky Navigation Clearance:** The bottom navigation bar reserves `72px` of vertical height plus iOS safe area inset (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`), ensuring scrollable content clears interactive zones seamlessly.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids dark or harsh drop shadows in favor of **warm, ambient-tinted layers and physical micro-offsets**:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Layer:* `#FAF8F5` (Warm Cream).
  - *Resting Cards & Modules:* `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1px` structural boundary (`#EFEAE2`) and a tinted ambient drop: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(224, 90, 54, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 43, 51, 0.04)`.
  - *Interactive Nodes & Badges:* Raised elements incorporate a distinct bottom edge bevel (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 0 #C84624` on primary items), giving components a tangible, pressable arcade feel.
  - *Overlays & Celebratory Sheets:* Soft backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) tinted with `rgba(31, 30, 36, 0.4)` to focus visual attention on modal rewards and cultural passport cards.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts smooth, welcoming curves that emphasize playfulness and touch ergonomics:

- **Cards & Modal Sheets:** Rendered with `rounded-xl` (`24px`) to establish friendly modular containers for questions, lore cards, and passport stamps.
- **Interactive Action Buttons & Badges:** Use full capsule/pill shapes (`roundedness: 9999px`) to invite thumb taps and reinforce the game-like interaction pattern.
- **Chips, Counters, & Node Containers:** Use `rounded-lg` (`12px` to `16px`) for structured compactness.
- **Cultural Texture Motifs:** Subtle Kaili-inspired geometric diamond accents are integrated inside badges, divider flourishes, and passport headers as smooth vector vectorizations, never intrusive.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Arcade Button:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), Terracotta Ember background (`#E05A36`) with a dimensional `3px` solid bottom shadow (`#C84624`). Active state translates `2px` downward with shadow reduced to `1px` for authentic physical feedback. Text is centered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` `label-lg` white.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#E2DBD0`, text in `#1F1E24`. Active state uses light cream fill (`#F5F2EB`).
- **Culture Action Button:** Sulawesi Indigo (`#6366F1`) with bottom bevel (`#4F46E5`), used specifically for opening cultural lore, textile deep-dives, and audio pronunciation guides.

### Badges & Gamification Chips
- **Streak Flame Chip:** Rounded capsule (`rounded-full`), pale warm orange fill (`#FFF7ED`), warm orange border (`#FED7AA`), featuring an animated flame icon alongside the day count in `#C84624` (`label-md`).
- **XP Tracker:** Rounded capsule with amber fill (`#FEF3C7`), gold star asset, and high-contrast numerical tracking (`#B45309`).
- **Level Capsule:** Slate pill (`#2D2B33`) with crisp white level text and an internal micro progress-ring.

### Progress & Mastery Bars
- **Dual-Track Language / Culture Mastery Bar:** A `12px` height track (`#EFEAE2` background, `rounded-full`). Filled with a segmented duo-gradient: Terracotta (`#E05A36`) for language proficiency and Indigo (`#6366F1`) for cultural unlocks, featuring a gentle shimmer animation upon level up.

### Cards & Lesson Nodes
- **Lesson Path Node:** Circular `64px × 64px` interactive nodes connected by a dotted baseline path. Complete nodes are terracotta with a white checkmark; active nodes feature a pulsing outer ring; locked nodes remain muted cream (`#EFEAE2`) with a subtle lock icon.
- **Interactive Exercise Card:** Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`), `1.5px` warm slate border (`#EFEAE2`), `20px` internal padding, containing audio trigger buttons (`#FAF8F5` surface with speaker icon), large vernacular phrases (e.g., "Tabe", "Nggembe"), and tactile multiple-choice options.
- **Culture Passport Card:** High-sheen card with subtle Central Sulawesi textile patterns in 4% opacity watermark, badge slot holders, audio recording buttons, and regional origin stamps.

### Selection Controls (Radio / Choice Tiles)
- Choice tiles are full-width cards with `16px` border-radius, resting on a `#FFFFFF` fill with `#EFEAE2` border. Selected state transitions to `#FFF7ED` surface with `#E05A36` `2px` border and bold text.

### Navigation & Celebratory Sheets
- **Bottom Navigation Bar:** Floating docked bar (`16px` lateral margins), white frosted surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), containing 4 icon-label pairs: *Home*, *Belajar (Path)*, *Paspor (Culture)*, and *Profil*. Active tab is highlighted with a terracotta dot indicator and bold label.
- **Success Celebration Bottom Sheet:** Anchored to bottom, `#FFFFFF` with `28px` top radius, celebratory emerald header (`#10B981`), XP floating pill count, and a full-width "Lanjut" (Continue) primary button.
